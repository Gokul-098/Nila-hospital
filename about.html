About Nila Child Care

A children's hospital in Dharmapuri

Care built around children and their parents

Nila is the Tamil word for the moon, a familiar comfort in a child's night sky. Nila Child Care is a hospital in Dharmapuri dedicated to the health of children.

We aim to make every visit calm for the child and clear for the parent. You can reach us by phone or WhatsApp to ask questions or book a visit before you come.
